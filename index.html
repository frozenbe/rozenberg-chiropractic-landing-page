Original Medicare coverage

Coverage is limited to eligible spinal manipulation.

Medicare Part B may cover manual manipulation of the spine when medically necessary to correct a vertebral subluxation.

  • Deductibles and coinsurance may apply.
  • Medicare Advantage and supplemental plan benefits can differ.
  • Exams, X-rays, massage therapy, acupuncture, and maintenance care are generally not covered when furnished by a chiropractor.
